Privacy Policy

Brain&Bot QuickBooks Integration
Effective date: 2026-05-02

This Privacy Policy explains how the Brain&Bot QuickBooks Integration collects, uses, and protects information when used with QuickBooks Online.

The Brain&Bot QuickBooks Integration is a private internal business application. It is used only by authorized users within our organization and is not offered as a public marketplace application.

Information we access

  • QuickBooks Online company information
  • Accounting and financial data explicitly authorized by the connected user
  • OAuth tokens and connection metadata required to maintain the integration

How information is collected

  • Via OAuth 2.0 authorization with Intuit QuickBooks Online
  • From API responses returned by QuickBooks Online after authorization

How information is used

  • To connect to QuickBooks Online
  • To retrieve, display, process, and synchronize authorized business data
  • To maintain, operate, and support the internal integration

Data protection

  • Access is restricted to authorized users only
  • Credentials, tokens, and related integration data are stored securely
  • Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect data

Data sharing

  • We do not sell authorized QuickBooks data
  • Data is shared only with service providers or infrastructure required to operate the internal application, where applicable

Your choices

  • You may revoke the app’s access through QuickBooks or by contacting us
  • Disconnecting the integration may stop related functionality
